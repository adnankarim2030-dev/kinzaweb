From the Kingdom to the Heart of Pakistan

Established under the visionary leadership of Al Jameel International, Kinza was born in the heart of the Saudi Arabian Kingdom in 2022. Our journey began with a mission to create a locally-owned refreshment brand that rivals global standards.

Today, that legacy of excellence has expanded beyond borders. By blending our premium heritage with the vibrant local culture of Pakistan, we are bringing a new era of refreshment to a whole new audience.
